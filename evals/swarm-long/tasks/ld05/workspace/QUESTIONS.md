# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md. Q2 2026 = slot dates 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30; first half = 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** What was the no-show rate in Q2 2026 (slots from April to June), as a percentage?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q1: 9.15`.

**Q2.** What was the billed revenue in EUR for slots in March 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q3.** Which clinician had the most completed appointments in May 2026? Ties go to the lowest clinician_id.

Format: the clinician_id, e.g. `Q3: P01`.

**Q4.** How many distinct patients aged 65 or over had at least 3 completed appointments in the first half of 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q5.** What is the median booking lead time in days of the completed `new_patient` appointments of the first half of 2026? Lead time = slot date minus the date of booked_at, in calendar days. With an even count take the mean of the two middle values.

Format: a number with 1 decimal, e.g. `Q5: 12.0`.

**Q6.** Which clinic had the highest no-show rate in the first half of 2026?

Format: the clinic_id.

**Q7.** How many patients had two or more no-shows (late cancellations included) in the first half of 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q8.** What was the billed revenue in EUR in Q2 2026 from patients of tariff class S?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.
