# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md. Months are calendar months.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** What was the total MRR in USD in June 2026?

Format: USD with 2 decimals, no currency sign, e.g. `Q1: 98765.43`.

**Q2.** How many accounts were active in March 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q3.** How many accounts churned in April 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q4.** Net revenue retention: take the accounts active in January 2026; what is their combined MRR in June 2026 as a percentage of their combined MRR in January 2026?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q4: 101.37`.

**Q5.** Which country (accounts.csv `country`) had the largest absolute MRR increase in USD from January 2026 to June 2026?

Format: the two-letter country code as in accounts.csv.

**Q6.** What was the ARPA in USD of the enterprise segment in April 2026?

Format: USD with 2 decimals.

**Q7.** How many new accounts were there in Q2 2026 (new in April, May or June)?

Format: an integer.

**Q8.** What share of the June 2026 MRR came from annual plans?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q8: 31.40`.
