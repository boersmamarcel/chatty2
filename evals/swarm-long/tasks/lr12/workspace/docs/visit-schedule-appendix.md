### Glossary of working terms

- **Baseline**: the agreed reference state against which later changes are described.
- **Handover**: the transfer of responsibility from one team or person to another.
- **Artefact**: any document, file or record produced as part of the work.
- **Working day**: a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday at the relevant site.
- **Escalation**: the act of raising an unresolved matter to the next level of authority.

### Frequently asked questions

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. For completeness, Quinn Lindqvist confirmed that the style guide had been filed in the usual place.

**What if an owner leaves the organisation?**
The line manager nominates a successor within the usual handover period. In the interim, the finance team will keep using the existing informed consent forms and note any gaps.

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. In the interim, the communications team will keep using the existing audit trail and note any gaps.

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. Olga Grosz offered to archive the old drafts, which was welcomed by the group.
