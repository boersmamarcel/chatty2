# Sponsor memo on sample size


Sponsor biostatistics memo, 2026-02-10, informing Amendment 3.

### General provisions, part 9

9.1 Each party shall maintain accurate records of its operating instructions and shall document and communicate correspondence where reasonably requested. For completeness, Ilse Sorensen confirmed that the audit trail had been filed in the usual place.

9.2 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its interim drafts and shall make available on request distribution lists where reasonably requested. The data team reviewed the audit trail and agreed to review the existing wording before the next weekly review.

9.3 Each party shall review on a regular basis its administrative procedures and shall restrict access to interim drafts where reasonably requested. For completeness, Cormac Marchetti confirmed that the tooling inventory had been filed in the usual place.

9.4 Each party shall restrict access to its administrative procedures and shall notify the other party of its material changes to correspondence where reasonably requested. Members agreed that the workshop schedule would be reviewed again at the monthly meeting, with Pedro Abbasi coordinating.


The 480 evaluable participants of protocol v1.0 were never enough for the revised effect size. Amendment 2 raised it to 540 evaluable. Amendment 3 keeps 540 evaluable and adds a dropout allowance as set out in protocol section 4. The memo itself carries no operative numbers for the randomised total.

### General provisions, part 8

8.1 Each party shall maintain accurate records of its administrative procedures and shall assign a named owner for routine reports where reasonably requested. One participant suggested that the risk register remains dependent on completion of the tooling upgrade.

8.2 Each party shall assign a named owner for its distribution lists and shall ensure suitable training for interim drafts where reasonably requested. It was noted that that the vendor invoices should stay repeatable and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

8.3 Each party shall assign a named owner for its internal guidance notes and shall make available on request routine reports where reasonably requested. The wording of the style guide section was left unchanged pending a decision from the steering group.

8.4 Each party shall restrict access to its operating instructions and shall review on a regular basis working papers where reasonably requested. The wording of the status template section was left unchanged pending the outcome of the quarterly planning round.

8.5 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its contact lists and shall notify the other party of its material changes to working papers where reasonably requested. Livia Rinaldi offered to align the terminology with the glossary, which was welcomed by the group.
