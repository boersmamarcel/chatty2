# IRB approval letter for Amendment 3


Letter from the central Institutional Review Board, dated 2026-03-09, confirming approval of Amendment 3.

### General provisions, part 2

2.1 Each party shall make available on request its reference material and shall ensure suitable training for routine reports where reasonably requested. The chair reminded attendees that that the handover notes should stay proportionate and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

2.2 Each party shall ensure suitable training for its supporting schedules and shall make available on request reference material where reasonably requested. The proportionate approach to the informed consent forms was considered sufficient for now, subject to the updated organisation chart.

2.3 Each party shall ensure suitable training for its interim drafts and shall review on a regular basis reference material where reasonably requested. In the interim, the facilities team will keep using the existing audit trail and note any gaps.

2.4 Each party shall maintain accurate records of its administrative procedures and shall make available on request contact lists where reasonably requested. For completeness, Gideon Brandvold confirmed that the sign-off workflow had been filed in the usual place.

2.5 Each party shall maintain accurate records of its interim drafts and shall assign a named owner for interim drafts where reasonably requested. The transparent approach to the audit trail was considered sufficient for now, subject to the updated organisation chart.

2.6 Each party shall keep confidential its reference material and shall maintain accurate records of contact lists where reasonably requested. The lightweight approach to the budget tracking was considered sufficient for now, subject to the outcome of the quarterly planning round.


The IRB approved Amendment 3 (protocol v4.0) at its meeting of 5 March 2026; the approval date is 2026-03-05 and the letter is issued four days later. Sites may implement Amendment 3 from the approval date. The approved protocol is the version signed by the sponsor on 2026-02-17.
