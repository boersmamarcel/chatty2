# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** How many valid orders have an order date in February 2026?

Format: an integer, e.g. `Q1: 1234`.

**Q2.** What is the total net revenue in EUR of valid orders dated in Q1 2026?

Format: EUR, 2 decimals, no currency sign, e.g. `Q2: 123456.78`.

**Q3.** Which region had the highest net revenue in EUR in March 2026?

Format: the region name exactly as in stores.csv.

**Q4.** What was the refund rate of the `app` channel in Q1 2026, i.e. refunds in EUR divided by gross revenue in EUR of valid app orders, as a percentage?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, no % sign needed, e.g. `Q4: 7.25`.

**Q5.** Which customer had the highest net revenue in EUR in Q1 2026? Ties go to the lowest customer_id.

Format: the customer_id, e.g. `Q5: C0001`.

**Q6.** How many valid orders dated in Q1 2026 were fully refunded?

Format: an integer.

**Q7.** What is the median gross amount in EUR of the valid orders of store S06 dated in Q1 2026? With an even number of orders, take the mean of the two middle values.

Format: EUR with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q7: 64.10`.

**Q8.** Which stores had a March 2026 net revenue in EUR more than 10% above their February 2026 net revenue?

Format: the store_ids, comma-separated, e.g. `Q8: S01, S02`.
