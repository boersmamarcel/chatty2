# Questions

All questions use README.md including its changelog (part D) and footnotes. Months are billing months (B.3).

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** How many billed HOME and EU voice minutes were there in billing month May 2026, over all subscribers?

Format: minutes with 1 decimal, e.g. `Q1: 1234.5`.

**Q2.** What was the total overage charge in EUR in billing month April 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q3.** What was the total roaming charge in EUR in billing month May 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q4.** How many subscribers exceeded their data allowance in billing month June 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q5.** What was the total of monthly fees in EUR in billing month June 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q6.** What was the average invoice total in EUR of the business-segment subscribers billed for May 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q7.** How many overage megabytes were billed in total in billing month June 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q8.** How many messages were charged in billing month April 2026: out-of-allowance HOME/EU messages plus all ROW messages?

Format: an integer.
