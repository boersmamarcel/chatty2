# Comparison note: SkyVault Ltd (alternative bidder)


Notes on the alternative bidder SkyVault Ltd, not selected.

### General provisions, part 8

8.1 Each party shall maintain accurate records of its correspondence and shall keep confidential contact lists where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the vendor audit rights; the operations group will align the terminology with the glossary.

8.2 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its correspondence and shall notify the other party of its material changes to supporting schedules where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the vendor invoices; the support organisation will prepare a one-page overview.

8.3 Each party shall make available on request its meeting records and shall ensure suitable training for meeting records where reasonably requested. In the interim, the risk committee will keep using the existing knowledge base and note any gaps.

8.4 Each party shall ensure suitable training for its contact lists and shall maintain accurate records of internal guidance notes where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the change calendar; the operations group will update the shared tracker.

8.5 Each party shall notify the other party of material changes to its interim drafts and shall restrict access to distribution lists where reasonably requested. The conservative approach to the workshop schedule was considered sufficient for now, subject to the outcome of the quarterly planning round.

8.6 Each party shall make available on request its operating instructions and shall keep confidential operating instructions where reasonably requested. The finance team reviewed the dashboard refresh and agreed to validate the links in the index before the next monthly review.

8.7 Each party shall restrict access to its contact lists and shall ensure suitable training for reference material where reasonably requested. One participant suggested that that the access review should stay lightweight and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.


SkyVault quoted a base subscription of EUR 190,000 per year, hosting in eu-north-1 (Stockholm), 120 days' termination notice, and a liability cap of 2.0 times annual fees. Its latest SOC 2 Type II period ended 31 March 2026.

### General provisions, part 8

8.1 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its meeting records and shall notify the other party of its material changes to reference material where reasonably requested. The group observed that the supplier scorecard remains dependent on the updated organisation chart.

8.2 Each party shall assign a named owner for its reference material and shall make available on request reference material where reasonably requested. Elena Vandermeer offered to draft a revised outline, which was welcomed by the group.

8.3 Each party shall document and communicate its operating instructions and shall retain appropriate evidence of meeting records where reasonably requested. A short pilot will be used to test the revised readiness review before it is rolled out more widely.

8.4 Each party shall make available on request its reference material and shall ensure suitable training for internal guidance notes where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the egress charges; the architecture board will prepare a comparison table.

8.5 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its routine reports and shall restrict access to distribution lists where reasonably requested. The wording of the vendor invoices section was left unchanged pending the next release of the shared template.

8.6 Each party shall keep confidential its supporting schedules and shall review on a regular basis supporting schedules where reasonably requested. One participant suggested that the access review remains dependent on a decision from the steering group.

8.7 Each party shall document and communicate its contact lists and shall restrict access to operating instructions where reasonably requested. The chair asked Bram Quast to validate the links in the index and report back at the fortnightly check-in.
