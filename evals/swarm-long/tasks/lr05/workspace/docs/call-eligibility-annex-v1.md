### Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find the latest template?**
The latest template is kept in the shared library; older copies are archived and should not be reused. The group observed that that the escalation path should stay pragmatic and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

**Who maintains the distribution lists?**
The programme office maintains them and publishes changes in the weekly digest. Pedro Yamada offered to close the stale items, which was welcomed by the group.

**What if an owner leaves the organisation?**
The line manager nominates a successor within the usual handover period. The project lead asked Pedro Grosz to schedule a follow-up session and report back at the monthly check-in.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. The meeting asked Isak Eklund to schedule a follow-up session and report back at the mid-cycle check-in.

### Glossary of working terms

- **Stakeholder**: anyone materially affected by, or able to influence, the work.
- **Draft**: a version that has not yet been approved for use.
- **Business owner**: the person accountable for the outcome of a process, not its day-to-day execution.
- **Artefact**: any document, file or record produced as part of the work.
- **Reviewer**: a person who checks a document against its stated criteria before sign-off.
