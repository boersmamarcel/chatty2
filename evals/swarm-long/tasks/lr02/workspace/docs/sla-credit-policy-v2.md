### Frequently asked questions

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. The platform group reviewed the status template and agreed to update the shared tracker before the next fortnightly review.

**How are conflicting instructions handled?**
Raise the conflict with the document owner, who records the decision in the change log. The incremental approach to the readiness review was considered sufficient for now, subject to feedback from the regional offices.

**Where do I find the latest template?**
The latest template is kept in the shared library; older copies are archived and should not be reused. The wording of the backlog grooming section was left unchanged pending access to the shared drive.

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. No objections were raised to the proposal on the pager rotations; the legal desk will archive the old drafts.


Credit for a payment outage exceeding 90 minutes: 15% of the affected customer's monthly platform fee. Credits are computed on the sum of the monthly platform fees of all affected customers. Outages of 90 minutes or less earn 5%.
