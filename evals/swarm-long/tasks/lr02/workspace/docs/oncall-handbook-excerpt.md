The line manager nominates a successor within the usual handover period. The finance team reviewed the workshop schedule and agreed to prepare a one-page overview before the next mid-cycle review.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. The consistent approach to the knowledge base was considered sufficient for now, subject to the outcome of the quarterly planning round.

**Is there a standard subject line for status mails?**

### Glossary of working terms

- **Baseline**: the agreed reference state against which later changes are described.
- **Artefact**: any document, file or record produced as part of the work.
- **Handover**: the transfer of responsibility from one team or person to another.
- **Escalation**: the act of raising an unresolved matter to the next level of authority.
- **Register**: a maintained list of items together with their status and owner.
