### Frequently asked questions

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. Members agreed that the knowledge base would be reviewed again at the mid-cycle meeting, with Farid Castellan coordinating.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. The chair asked Isak Haugen to prepare a comparison table and report back at the mid-cycle check-in.

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. Several members pointed out that the escalation path remains dependent on availability of the reviewers.

**Who maintains the distribution lists?**
The programme office maintains them and publishes changes in the weekly digest. The data team reviewed the readiness review and agreed to review the existing wording before the next fortnightly review.
