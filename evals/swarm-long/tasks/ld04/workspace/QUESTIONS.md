# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md. Q3 2026 = 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 (for weeks: weeks whose Sunday falls in that range).

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** How many payable hours have a work date in August 2026?

Format: hours with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q1: 12345.50`.

**Q2.** What was the labour cost in EUR of employee E017 in July 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q3.** How many overtime hours were there in total in the weeks belonging to Q3 2026 (July to September)?

Format: hours with 2 decimals.

**Q4.** Which department had the highest labour cost in September 2026?

Format: the department name as in departments.csv.

**Q5.** How many employees have at least one approved entry (current version) dated after their termination date?

Format: an integer.

**Q6.** How many payable hours were booked on project PRJ-ORION with a work date in Q3 2026?

Format: hours with 2 decimals.

**Q7.** What was the average hourly rate in effect on 2026-09-01 over the hourly employees employed on that day (hired on or before it, not terminated before it)?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q8.** What was the total holiday premium in EUR for work dates in Q3 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.
