# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** What was the total electricity consumption in kWh of site S03 in September 2026?

Format: kWh with 2 decimals.

**Q2.** What share of the total consumption of all sites in September 2026 was peak consumption?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q2: 61.25`.

**Q3.** What is the September 2026 energy bill in EUR of site S05?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q4.** Which site had the highest consumption in a single site hour in September 2026?

Format: the site_id.

**Q5.** How many local September 2026 hours of site S06 have no valid reading?

Format: an integer.

**Q6.** On which local date was the combined consumption of all sites highest? (Each site's intervals go to its own local date.)

Format: the date as YYYY-MM-DD.

**Q7.** How many kWh of the September 2026 consumption of all sites come from estimated readings (current quality `E`)?

Format: kWh with 2 decimals.

**Q8.** What was the average daily consumption in kWh of site S01 on the Saturdays and Sundays of September 2026 (local dates)?

Format: kWh with 2 decimals.
