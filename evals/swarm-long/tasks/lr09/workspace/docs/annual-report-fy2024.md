### Frequently asked questions

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. Mina Vandermeer offered to archive the old drafts, which was welcomed by the group.

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. In the interim, the risk committee will keep using the existing regulatory filings and note any gaps.

**Where do I find the latest template?**
The latest template is kept in the shared library; older copies are archived and should not be reused. A short pilot will be used to test the revised capacity forecast before it is rolled out more widely.

**Is there a standard subject line for status mails?**
Yes: the programme name, the word Status and the reporting period. There was general agreement that that the supervisory contacts should stay transparent and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.
