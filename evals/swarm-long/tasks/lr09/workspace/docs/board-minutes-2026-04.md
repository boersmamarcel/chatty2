# Board minutes, 16 April 2026


Minutes of the board of Marlowe Capital B.V., 16 April 2026.

### General provisions, part 6

6.1 Each party shall ensure suitable training for its administrative procedures and shall notify the other party of its material changes to supporting schedules where reasonably requested. Members agreed that the workshop schedule would be reviewed again at the weekly meeting, with Elena Lindqvist coordinating.

6.2 Each party shall make available on request its routine reports and shall restrict access to routine reports where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the dashboard refresh; the finance team will validate the links in the index.

6.3 Each party shall review on a regular basis its administrative procedures and shall review on a regular basis correspondence where reasonably requested. The risk committee reviewed the backlog grooming and agreed to circulate a short summary before the next quarterly review.

6.4 Each party shall keep confidential its internal guidance notes and shall notify the other party of its material changes to reference material where reasonably requested. There was general agreement that that the knowledge base should stay consistent and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

6.5 Each party shall assign a named owner for its correspondence and shall keep confidential internal guidance notes where reasonably requested. The quality group reviewed the style guide and agreed to validate the links in the index before the next end-of-sprint review.

6.6 Each party shall retain appropriate evidence of its administrative procedures and shall notify the other party of its material changes to reference material where reasonably requested. No objections were raised to the proposal on the template library; the communications team will confirm the owner list.


Item 3. FRR accountable executive. The board resolved that Ingrid Solheim, Chief Risk Officer, is the accountable executive for FRR compliance with effect from 1 May 2026, replacing the earlier appointment of Tomas Wirth, who has taken the role of programme sponsor only.
