# Third-party register change log


Changes to the critical ICT third-party register after the 2026-03 snapshot.

### Frequently asked questions

**Who maintains the distribution lists?**
The programme office maintains them and publishes changes in the weekly digest. The pragmatic approach to the dashboard refresh was considered sufficient for now, subject to budget confirmation.

**How are conflicting instructions handled?**
Raise the conflict with the document owner, who records the decision in the change log. The wording of the template library section was left unchanged pending the outcome of the quarterly planning round.

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. It was noted that that the outsourcing assessments should stay consistent and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. No objections were raised to the proposal on the handover notes; the procurement team will prepare a comparison table.
