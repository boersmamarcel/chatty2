### Glossary of working terms

- **Baseline**: the agreed reference state against which later changes are described.
- **Record**: information created or received that is kept as evidence of an activity.
- **Escalation**: the act of raising an unresolved matter to the next level of authority.
- **Draft**: a version that has not yet been approved for use.
- **Stakeholder**: anyone materially affected by, or able to influence, the work.

### Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find the latest template?**
The latest template is kept in the shared library; older copies are archived and should not be reused. The wording of the resilience testing section was left unchanged pending the holiday calendar.

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. The pragmatic approach to the template library was considered sufficient for now, subject to feedback from the regional offices.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. The risk committee reviewed the workshop schedule and agreed to review the existing wording before the next weekly review.

**How are conflicting instructions handled?**
