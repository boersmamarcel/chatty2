# Regulator FAQ on FRR (guidance, non-binding)


Published guidance of the regulator. Non-binding; the regulation text prevails.

### Frequently asked questions

**How are conflicting instructions handled?**
Raise the conflict with the document owner, who records the decision in the change log. For completeness, Zora Sorensen confirmed that the handover notes had been filed in the usual place.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. For completeness, Koen Bakker confirmed that the vendor invoices had been filed in the usual place.

**Who maintains the distribution lists?**
The programme office maintains them and publishes changes in the weekly digest. The wording of the tooling inventory section was left unchanged pending budget confirmation.

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. For completeness, Wim Underhill confirmed that the training plan had been filed in the usual place.
