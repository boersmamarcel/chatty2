Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. In the interim, the platform group will keep using the existing naming conventions and note any gaps.

**Is there a standard subject line for status mails?**
Yes: the programme name, the word Status and the reporting period. No objections were raised to the proposal on the audit trail; the procurement team will confirm the owner list.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. The group observed that that the reporting cadence should stay conservative and avoid adding new steps without a clear owner.

**What if an owner leaves the organisation?**
