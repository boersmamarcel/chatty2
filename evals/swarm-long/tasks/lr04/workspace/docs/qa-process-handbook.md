### Frequently asked questions

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. For completeness, Cormac Fontaine confirmed that the handover notes had been filed in the usual place.

**How long are drafts kept?**
Drafts are kept until the final version is published and then follow the normal archive routine. Farid Eriksen offered to prepare a comparison table, which was welcomed by the group.

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
Yes, provided a deputy has been named in advance and the minutes record this. One participant suggested that the sign-off workflow remains dependent on access to the shared drive.

**How are conflicting instructions handled?**
Raise the conflict with the document owner, who records the decision in the change log. In the interim, the support organisation will keep using the existing meeting rhythm and note any gaps.

### Glossary of working terms

- **Working day**: a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday at the relevant site.
- **Register**: a maintained list of items together with their status and owner.
- **Distribution list**: the set of recipients who receive a document by default.
- **Record**: information created or received that is kept as evidence of an activity.
- **Draft**: a version that has not yet been approved for use.
