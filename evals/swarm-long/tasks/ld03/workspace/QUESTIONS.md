# Questions

All questions use the definitions in README.md. Q3 2026 = ship dates 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** How many shipments with a ship date in August 2026 have the current status `delivered`?

Format: an integer.

**Q2.** What was the on-time rate of carrier DHX in Q3 2026 (ship dates July to September), as a percentage?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q2: 88.10`.

**Q3.** Which carrier had the lowest on-time rate in Q3 2026?

Format: the carrier_code.

**Q4.** What was the total freight cost in EUR of DC SIN1's shipments with a ship date in September 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q5.** What was the average number of transit days of the delivered shipments of DC ATL1 with a ship date in Q3 2026?

Format: a number with 2 decimals.

**Q6.** Which destination country had the most late deliveries (delivered, not on time) among shipments with a ship date in Q3 2026? Ties go to the alphabetically first code.

Format: the dest_country code.

**Q7.** How many shipments are still in transit (current status `in_transit`) although they were shipped (shipped_at_utc) before 2026-09-20T00:00:00Z?

Format: an integer.

**Q8.** What was the total billable weight in kg of carrier UPG's shipments with a ship date in Q3 2026 (cancelled excluded)?

Format: an integer (kg).
