### General provisions, part 8

8.1 Each party shall notify the other party of material changes to its interim drafts and shall maintain accurate records of meeting records where reasonably requested. Yusuf Weiss offered to align the terminology with the glossary, which was welcomed by the group.

8.2 Each party shall review on a regular basis its distribution lists and shall assign a named owner for correspondence where reasonably requested. A short walkthrough will be used to test the revised archive migration before it is rolled out more widely.

8.3 Each party shall assign a named owner for its supporting schedules and shall review on a regular basis correspondence where reasonably requested. A short desk review will be used to test the revised egress charges before it is rolled out more widely.

8.5 Each party shall restrict access to its administrative procedures and shall assign a named owner for administrative procedures where reasonably requested. The steering group reviewed the knowledge base and agreed to align the terminology with the glossary before the next mid-cycle review.
