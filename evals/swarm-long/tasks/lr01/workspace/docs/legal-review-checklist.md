### Frequently asked questions

**Who may approve a minor wording change?**
The document owner may approve editorial changes that do not alter meaning; anything else goes to the reviewers. Yusuf Dvorak offered to collect comments from the wider group, which was welcomed by the group.

**Is there a standard subject line for status mails?**
Yes: the programme name, the word Status and the reporting period. The incremental approach to the status template was considered sufficient for now, subject to completion of the tooling upgrade.

**What if an owner leaves the organisation?**

**Can a meeting be held without the chair?**
