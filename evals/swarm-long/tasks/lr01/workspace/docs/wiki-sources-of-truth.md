# Procurement wiki: sources of truth


Wiki policy page. The order of precedence for contract facts is: (1) the signed contract documents in their latest effective version, (2) later signed addenda, (3) vendor questionnaires, (4) wiki summaries. A lower-ranked source never overrides a higher one, and a questionnaire answer never overrides an executed addendum.
