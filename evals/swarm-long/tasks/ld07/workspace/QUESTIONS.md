# Questions

All questions use README.md including its changelog (part D) and footnotes. "Claims in scope" is defined in B.2.

Write each answer on its own line `Qn: <answer>` at the top of report.md, in the format stated with the question.

**Q1.** How many claims in scope have an accident period in Q2 2026 and the line of business `home_premium`?

Format: an integer.

**Q2.** What is the total net paid in EUR of the claims in scope whose accident period is March 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q2: 123456.78`.

**Q3.** How many large losses are there among the claims in scope with an accident period in the first half of 2026 (January to June)?

Format: an integer.

**Q4.** What is the average net paid in EUR per closed non-CAT claim of line `motor` with an accident period in Q1 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.

**Q5.** What is the recovery ratio of line `commercial_motor` for accident periods in Q2 2026: total recoveries in EUR divided by total indemnity in EUR, as a percentage?

Format: a percentage with 2 decimals, e.g. `Q5: 12.34`.

**Q6.** How many out-of-cover claims (B.2 rule 4) have a loss date in the first half of 2026?

Format: an integer.

**Q7.** What is the mean reporting delay in days of the non-CAT claims in scope of line `home` with an accident period in the first half of 2026?

Format: days with 2 decimals.

**Q8.** What is the total of expense bookings in EUR on CAT claims in scope with an accident period in the first half of 2026?

Format: EUR with 2 decimals.
