# Audit committee minutes, 2026-06-12 (extract)

Present: CFO, finance controller, head of internal audit, two non-executive
members.

3. Split purchases. Internal audit reported that last year's two breaches of
   the splitting rule were both found by the external auditor, not by our own
   checks. The committee asked finance to run a first-digit (Benford) screen
   and a threshold screen on every quarter's payments from Q3 2026 onwards.

4. Consulting spend. Consulting spend rose 40% year on year. The committee
   asked that any new consulting vendor without a statement of work be flagged
   to the controller on its first invoice.

Actions: finance controller to report the first Q3 screen to the committee
at its October meeting.
