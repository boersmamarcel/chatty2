# Procurement policy (v3, effective 2026-01-01)

## 1. Approval limits

| Amount per invoice | Who approves |
|---|---|
| up to 4,999.99 | the budget holder |
| 5,000.00 to 24,999.99 | the budget holder and the finance controller |
| 25,000.00 and above | the CFO, after three written quotes |

## 2. Splitting purchases

A purchase may not be split into several invoices, orders or payments to keep
each part under an approval limit. Invoices from one vendor for the same
project within 30 days count as one purchase for the limits in section 1.

## 3. Duplicate payments

Accounts payable checks every payment run for duplicates (same vendor, amount
and date). A duplicate that was paid must be recovered from the vendor within
60 days and reported to the finance controller.

## 4. Breaches

A suspected breach of section 2 is reported to the finance controller within
five working days. The controller decides whether internal audit reviews it.
The budget holder is not told who reported it.
